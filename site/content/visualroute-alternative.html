VisualRoute alternative: Route Map, free and open source

Route Map is a free, open source visual traceroute for Windows, macOS and Linux. It places each router by its operator's own hostname first, checks every placement against the round trip, and shows where each hop's location came from. No account, no telemetry.

About VisualRoute

VisualRoute is a commercial traceroute tool from Visualware Inc. It runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 114 and is sold as a perpetual licence in Personal, Advanced, Business and SupportPro editions, priced through a configurator on its pricing page, with optional annual support and a 15-day trial1,2. The current release is 15.0a; its release notes give no date3.

What Route Map does not do

Route Map draws no submarine cables and labels no exchange points: the cable data is sold under licence, and PeeringDB's terms do not allow bundling its prefix list. Its Windows and macOS builds are not code-signed yet. It traces from your computer or from a server you already reach with ssh, and runs no measurement servers of its own. Its port test is a reachability check, not a port scanner.
